## 🎖️ National Purple Heart Benefits

These benefits apply to **all Purple Heart recipients** regardless of what state you live in.

> These are benefits specific to the Purple Heart award. Purple Heart recipients also remain eligible for all standard VA and veteran benefits.

### 🏥 Health Care

- **VA Priority Group 3** ,  Purple Heart recipients are automatically placed in VA Priority Group 3, giving higher-priority access to medical care regardless of disability rating or income.
- **No VA Copays** ,  Recipients are exempt from copayments for VA medical treatment and hospitalization.
- **Expedited Claims Processing** ,  The Purple Heart serves as supporting evidence for VA disability claims, reducing the burden of proof required.
- **Veterans Health Identification Card (VHIC)** ,  Enroll in VA health care to receive a VHIC marked **"PURPLE HEART"** — required for commissary, exchange, and MWR access. ([Get a VHIC](https://www.va.gov/health-care/get-health-id-card/))
- **Special Monthly Compensation (SMC)** ,  Additional tax-free monthly pay for severe service-connected disabilities such as limb loss, blindness, aid & attendance, or housebound status. ([VA SMC Rates](https://www.va.gov/disability/compensation-rates/special-monthly-compensation-rates/))
- **VA Caregiver Support Program** ,  Training, respite care, and (for eligible veterans) a monthly stipend through the Program of Comprehensive Assistance for Family Caregivers (PCAFC). ([VA Caregiver Support](https://www.va.gov/family-and-caregiver-benefits/health-and-wellness/))

### 🎓 Education

- **Full GI Bill Benefits** ,  Purple Heart recipients awarded on or after September 11, 2001 are eligible for 36 months of 100%-level Post-9/11 GI Bill benefits regardless of length of service. ([VA GI Bill](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/post-9-11/))
- **Yellow Ribbon Program** ,  Additional support covering tuition and fees beyond standard GI Bill limits. ([VA Yellow Ribbon](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/post-9-11/yellow-ribbon-program/))
- **Transfer Post-9/11 GI Bill Benefits** ,  Purple Heart recipients at the 100% benefit level may transfer unused education benefits to a spouse or dependent children. ([Transfer GI Bill](https://www.va.gov/education/transfer-post-9-11-gi-bill-benefits/))
- [MOPH Scholarship Program](https://www.purpleheart.org/ScholarshipProgram) ,  Scholarships for Purple Heart recipients and their spouses, children, and grandchildren.

### 💼 Employment

- **10-Point Federal Hiring Preference** ,  Purple Heart recipients with an honorable discharge receive a permanent 10-point preference on federal job applications. Must submit [SF-15 Form](https://www.opm.gov/forms/pdf_fill/sf15.pdf) to claim. ([OPM Veterans Preference](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/veterans-services/vet-guide-for-hr-professionals/))

### 🏠 Housing & Loans

- **VA Home Loan Funding Fee Exemption** ,  Purple Heart recipients on active duty or receiving disability compensation are exempt from the VA home loan funding fee. ([VA Home Loans](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/))
- **Specially Adapted Housing Grant** ,  Fully disabled recipients may be eligible for grants to modify their home to accommodate their disability. ([VA SAH Grant](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/adaptive-housing-grants/))

### 🛒 Shopping & Recreation

- **Commissary & Exchange Access** ,  All Purple Heart recipients can shop at military commissaries and exchanges for cost savings. ([Defense Commissary Agency](https://www.commissaries.com/))
- **How to Access Commissary & Exchange (Step-by-Step)** ,  (1) Enroll in VA health care and obtain a VHIC marked "PURPLE HEART." (2) Visit the installation visitor center with your VHIC or REAL ID for a background check and electronic eligibility verification. (3) Use the same credential at the gate for future visits — enrollment is typically valid 1–3 years. ([Commissary Extended Eligibility](https://commissaries.com/extended-eligibility)) | ([VA/DOD Installation Access](https://news.va.gov/press-room/va-and-dod-announce-updates-to-improve-veteran-and-caregiver-access-to-installations/))
- **Commissary Credit/Debit Card User Fee** ,  Purple Heart patrons pay a small user fee on credit and signature debit purchases at commissaries (~1.43% credit, ~0.36% PIN debit). Cash and MILSTAR are fee-free.
- **MWR Lodging & Retail Access** ,  Access to Morale, Welfare, and Recreation retail and lodging facilities. ([MWR](https://www.militaryonesource.mil/recreation-travel-shopping/))
- **National Parks Free Access** ,  Veterans with service-connected disabilities (including many Purple Heart recipients) qualify for a free lifetime America the Beautiful pass. ([National Parks Service](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/fees/passes.htm))
- **TSA PreCheck Reimbursement** ,  Veterans with a service-connected disability rating may be reimbursed for TSA PreCheck application fees. ([TSA PreCheck for Veterans](https://www.tsa.gov/precheck))

### 💰 Financial

- **Combat Related Special Compensation (CRSC)** ,  Tax-free monthly pay for military retirees with combat-related disabilities, paid in addition to retirement pay. ([DFAS CRSC](https://www.dfas.mil/retiredmilitary/disability/crsc/))
- **Concurrent Retirement and Disability Pay (CRDP)** ,  Military retirees with a 50%+ VA disability rating may receive full retired pay and VA disability compensation without offset. No separate application required — coordinated automatically by VA and DFAS. ([DFAS CRDP](https://www.dfas.mil/retiredmilitary/disability/crsc-crpd/))
- **VA Disability Compensation** ,  Monthly tax-free compensation based on service-connected disability rating. ([VA Disability](https://www.va.gov/disability/))
- **Dependency and Indemnity Compensation (DIC)** ,  Tax-free monthly benefit for surviving spouses and children when a service-connected death occurs. ([VA DIC](https://www.va.gov/disability/dependency-indemnity-compensation/))
- **VA Burial & Memorial Benefits** ,  Free burial in a VA national cemetery (when space is available), government headstone or marker, and perpetual care. Burial allowances may also apply. ([VA Burial Benefits](https://www.va.gov/burials-memorials/))
- **Arlington National Cemetery In-Ground Burial** ,  Purple Heart recipients are eligible for in-ground burial at Arlington National Cemetery, subject to eligibility rules and available space. ([Arlington Eligibility](https://www.arlingtoncemetery.mil/))

### 🎖️ MOPH Membership Benefits

- **$2,500 Accidental Death & Dismemberment** ,  No-cost AD&D benefit for MOPH members. Register your beneficiary at [mybenefits.ailife.com](https://mybenefits.ailife.com) (access code: SGP9O). ([MOPH Benefits](https://www.purpleheart.org/Benefits))
- **Free VA Claims Assistance** ,  MOPH National Service Officers (DSVS) provide free help with VA claims, especially combat-injury, TBI, and PTSD claims. ([MOPH National](https://www.purpleheart.org))
- **MOPH Programs** ,  Homeless Veterans Program, Legislative advocacy, Youth Services, Software Developer Training, and POW Program. ([MOPH Programs](https://www.purpleheart.org))
- **MOPH State Benefits Guide** ,  Downloadable list of Purple Heart benefits by state. ([MOPH State Benefits](https://www.purpleheart.org/Benefits))
- **Retroactive Purple Heart Applications** ,  Veterans who believe they qualify but were never formally awarded the medal can apply retroactively with help from MOPH or NVLSP. ([MOPH National](https://www.purpleheart.org)) | ([NVLSP](https://www.nvlsp.org/))

## Missouri

> **Missouri Purple Heart–Specific Benefits:** Free Purple Heart license plates, free Military Award Placard (parking), and (if enacted) property tax relief — see [Transportation & DMV Benefits](#-transportation--dmv-benefits) and [Pending Missouri Legislation](#-pending-missouri-legislation).

### 🏛️ Official State & Federal Agencies

- [Missouri Veterans Commission (MVC)](https://mvc.dps.mo.gov/) ,  State agency that aids veterans, dependents, and families with benefits, veterans homes, cemeteries, and claims assistance.
- [Missouri Veterans Benefits & Resources Portal](https://veteranbenefits.mo.gov/) ,  One-stop portal for state and federal veteran benefits, resources, and VSO locator.
- [MO.gov Veterans Hub](https://www.mo.gov/veterans/) ,  Missouri's official gateway to employment, education, housing, crisis lines, and more.
- [VA St. Louis Regional Office](https://www.benefits.va.gov/stlouis/) ,  Administers compensation and Vocational Rehabilitation & Employment (VR&E) benefits for Missouri veterans.
- [Missouri Department of Mental Health – Veterans Resources](https://dmh.mo.gov/) ,  State mental health agency with veteran-specific programs and crisis services.
- [Missouri Military Resource Locator](https://myarmybenefits.us.army.mil/Benefit-Library/State/Territory-Benefits/Missouri) ,  Comprehensive Army benefits summary for Missouri service members and veterans.

### 📋 Benefits & Claims Assistance

- [MVC Veterans Service Officers (VSO) Locator](https://veteranbenefits.mo.gov/) ,  Free, trained VSOs who assist with compensation, pension, education, insurance, medical, and burial benefit claims.
- [Missouri Veterans Benefits Guide (PDF)](https://mvc.dps.mo.gov/docs/veterans-benefits-guide.pdf) ,  Printable guide to all Missouri state and federal veteran benefits.
- [Disabled American Veterans (DAV) Missouri](https://www.dav.org/) ,  Free professional assistance with VA claims, appeals, and benefits.
- [Paralyzed Veterans of America](https://pva.org/) ,  Specialized expertise for veterans with spinal cord injury or dysfunction, including benefits advocacy.
- [Missouri National Guard Family Assistance Centers](https://moguard.com/) ,  12 FACs statewide providing support to Guard families during deployments and emergencies.
- [MOPH National Service Officers](https://www.purpleheart.org) ,  Free VA claims assistance specializing in combat-wounded veterans, TBI, and PTSD claims.

### 🧠 Mental Health & Crisis Support

- [Veterans Crisis Line](https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/) ,  Call **988, then press 1**. Free, confidential support 24/7 for veterans in crisis.
- [Access Crisis Intervention (ACI) Hotline](https://dmh.mo.gov/) ,  Free behavioral health crisis services available statewide; reduces unnecessary hospitalizations.
- [Missouri DMH Central Resource Hub](https://dmh.mo.gov/) ,  Single point of contact for non-emergency behavioral health guidance; returns calls within 48–72 hours.
- [Give an Hour](https://giveanhour.org/) ,  Volunteer mental health professionals offering free, confidential services to post-9/11 veterans and families.
- [K9s on the Front Line](https://www.k9sonthefrontline.org/) ,  Provides trained service dogs at no cost to veterans affected by PTSD and/or TBI.
- [Camp Hope – Farmington, MO](https://camphopemo.org/) ,  Expenses-paid retreat for veterans wounded in the War on Terror; located in Farmington, MO.
- [Hope Project STL](https://hopeprojectstl.org/) ,  St. Louis-area nonprofit using equine-assisted programs to support veterans, first responders, and families.
- [Valor Recovery Program – North Kansas City](https://valorportal.com/) ,  Trauma processing and stress management for active duty, veterans, and first responders.
- [PsychArmor Institute](https://psycharmor.org/) ,  Free online courses helping civilians and providers better support military members and veterans.
- [Cohen Veterans Network – Missouri Clinics](https://www.cohenveteransnetwork.org/locations/) ,  No-cost mental health care for post-9/11 veterans and families at clinics in St. Louis, Cape Girardeau, and other locations.

### 🏠 Housing & Homelessness Prevention

- [HUD-VASH Program](https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/public_indian_housing/programs/hcv/vash) ,  Combines HUD housing vouchers with VA case management and clinical services for homeless veterans.
- [Missouri Housing Development Commission](https://www.mhdc.com/) ,  Affordable housing resources for all Missourians, including veterans.
- [Welcome Home – Columbia, MO](https://www.welcomehomemo.org/) ,  Mid-Missouri's only veteran-centered emergency shelter; provides meals, housing, case management, and wraparound services at no cost.
- [Veterans Community Project](https://www.veteranscommunityproject.org/) ,  Builds communities of tiny homes in Kansas City to provide housing stability for homeless veterans.
- [St. Michael's Veterans Center (SMVC)](https://stmichaelsveteranscenter.org/) ,  Support for veterans with employment, physical and mental health, trauma, addiction, and long-term housing stability.
- [MOVE: Missouri Veterans Endeavor – St. John, MO](https://www.move-stl.org/) ,  Long-term supportive housing and services for veterans and families in the St. Louis area.
- [Salvation Army SSVF Program](https://www.salvationarmyusa.org/) ,  Supportive Services for Veteran Families (SSVF): helps low-income veterans obtain and maintain stable housing.
- [Mid-Missouri Fisher House](https://www.fisherhouse.org/) ,  Free temporary lodging for families of veterans receiving hospital care.

### 🎓 Education

- [Missouri Returning Heroes Education Act](https://dhewd.mo.gov/) ,  Caps tuition at $50/credit hour at public Missouri colleges for eligible combat veterans (post-9/11).
- [Heros 2 Hives](https://extension.missouri.edu/programs/heroes-to-hives) ,  Heros to Hives is a program to help veterans get into bee keeping.
- [War Veterans' Survivor Grant Program](https://dhewd.mo.gov/) ,  Annual grants for children (under 25) and spouses of veterans killed or severely disabled in combat since 9/11.
- **In-State Tuition for Recently Discharged Veterans** ,  Honorably discharged veterans who move to Missouri and declare intent to become a resident qualify for in-state tuition at public colleges and universities. ([MO Army Benefits – Education](https://myarmybenefits.us.army.mil/Benefit-Library/State/Territory-Benefits/Missouri))
- [University of Missouri Veterans Center](https://veteranscenter.missouri.edu/) ,  Resources, advising, and community for student veterans at Mizzou.
- [Missouri "Operation Recognition" Honorary Diplomas](https://dese.mo.gov/) ,  Honorary high school diplomas for veterans who left school to serve during wartime.
- [Missouri Purple Star School Program](https://dese.mo.gov/) ,  Recognizes military-friendly schools with dedicated liaisons and resources for military families.
- [Military Order of the Purple Heart Scholarship](https://www.purpleheart.org/ScholarshipProgram) ,  Scholarship for Purple Heart recipients and their spouses, children, and grandchildren.

### 💼 Employment

- [Show-Me Heroes Program](https://showmeheroes.mo.gov/) ,  50% wage reimbursement to employers who hire and train qualifying veterans; honorable discharge required.
- [Employer Support of the Guard and Reserve (ESGR)](https://www.esgr.mil/) ,  Resolves employer conflicts for Reserve Component service members; workplace support and education.
- [Feds Hire Vets](https://www.fedshirevets.gov/) ,  Federal employment resources and veteran hiring preference information.
- [Missouri Veterans Employment Preference](https://oa.mo.gov/) ,  Veterans receive 5-point (10-point for service-connected disabled) preference in Missouri state employment testing.
- [VA Vocational Rehabilitation & Employment (VR&E)](https://www.va.gov/careers-employment/vocational-rehabilitation/) ,  Job training, education, and employment services for veterans with service-connected disabilities.
- **Missouri Professional License Reciprocity for Veterans** ,  Military education, training, and experience count toward professional licensing requirements; extensions available for active-duty license holders. ([MO Office of Administration](https://oa.mo.gov/))

### 💰 Financial Assistance

- [Missouri Military Family Relief Fund (MMFRF)](https://mvc.dps.mo.gov/) ,  Financial assistance for qualified Missouri military members and their families.
- [Missouri Property Tax Credit (Circuit Breaker)](https://dor.mo.gov/) ,  Credit up to $1,100 for homeowners or $750 for renters who are 100% service-connected disabled veterans.
- **Missouri Income Tax Exemption for Military Retired Pay** ,  Military retired pay is fully deductible from Missouri adjusted gross income. ([MO DOR Military Tax Info](https://dor.mo.gov/taxation/military/))
- **Missouri Tax-Free VA Disability Compensation & DIC** ,  VA disability compensation and Dependency and Indemnity Compensation are not subject to Missouri income tax.
- **Missouri SBP/RCSBP/RSFPP Annuity Deduction** ,  Surviving spouses may deduct up to $47,633 of military Survivor Benefit Plan annuities on Missouri state taxes (2025 tax year). ([MO DOR Pension FAQ](https://dor.mo.gov/faq/pension/))
- [FISH – Greater Kansas City](https://fishkc.org/) ,  Emergency financial assistance for service members, veterans, and families in the KC metro area.
- [Semper Fi & America's Fund](https://semperfifund.org/) ,  Financial assistance and lifetime support for combat-wounded and critically ill veterans.
- [Purple Heart Foundation – Critical Assistance Grants](https://purpleheartfoundation.org/veteran-resource-center/) ,  One-time emergency grants for housing, vehicle repairs, medical costs, and other urgent needs for honorably discharged veterans.
- [Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP)](https://dss.mo.gov/fsd/liheap.htm) ,  Helps veterans and families pay home energy bills.

### ⚖️ Legal Services

- [University of Missouri School of Law Veterans Clinic](https://law.missouri.edu/clinics/) ,  Free legal assistance for veterans, including VA compensation claims and discharge upgrade applications.
- [National Veterans Legal Services Program (NVLSP)](https://www.nvlsp.org/) ,  Free representation for veterans with less-than-honorable discharges and PTSD/TBI-related appeals.

### 🚗 Transportation & DMV Benefits

- **Missouri Purple Heart License Plate** ,  One free set of specialty license plates for Missouri resident Purple Heart recipients. Requires official award documentation. ([MO Military Specialty Plates](https://dor.mo.gov/motor-vehicle/plates/personalized/specialty-plates.php))
- **Missouri Military Award Placard** ,  Free removable placard authorizing free parking in metered spaces, Missouri state park lots, and public college/university parking lots and garages (does not apply during special event parking). Available to Purple Heart plate holders. ([MO Military Award Placard](https://dor.mo.gov/motor-vehicle/plates/personalized/specialty-plates.php))
- **"VETERAN" Designation on Missouri Driver License/ID** ,  No additional cost; requires proof of honorable discharge. ([MO Veteran ID Designation](https://dor.mo.gov/driver-license/id-cards/veteran-designation.php))

### 🎣 Recreation & Outdoors

- **Missouri State Parks Camping Discount** ,  $2/n-night discount on camping fees for veterans and active-duty family members. Ask at time of payment. ([MO State Parks](https://mostateparks.com/))
- **Missouri Hunting & Fishing Permit Exemptions** ,  Veterans with 60%+ service-connected disability (many Purple Heart recipients qualify) may hunt and fish without most permits; must carry a VA eligibility letter or [MDC VA Carry Card](https://mdc.mo.gov/permits/veteran-benefits). Deer, turkey, and migratory bird permits still required.
- [Honor Flight of the Ozarks](https://www.honorflightoftheozarks.org/) ,  Free trips to Washington, D.C. war memorials for eligible era veterans (Springfield area hub).
- [Great River Honor Flight](https://www.greatriverhonorflight.com/) ,  Free Honor Flight trips for veterans in the Hannibal/Quincy tri-state area.
- [Honor Flight Network – Missouri Hubs](https://www.honorflight.org/hubs/) ,  Additional active Missouri hubs include Greater St. Louis Honor Flight and Heartland Honor Flight (Kansas City). *Note: Central Missouri Honor Flight suspended operations in September 2025.*

### 🏡 Long-Term Care & Memorial

- [Missouri Veterans Service Medallion](https://mvc.dps.mo.gov/) ,  Posthumous medallion available for eligible veterans through the Missouri Veterans Commission.
- [Jefferson Barracks National Cemetery](https://www.cem.va.gov/cems/nchp/jeffersonbarracks.asp) ,  One of the nation's oldest national cemeteries; burial place for veterans of all wars.
- [Missouri State Veterans Cemeteries](https://mvc.dps.mo.gov/cemetery.php) ,  Five state cemeteries (Springfield, Higginsville, Bloomfield, Ft. Leonard Wood, Jacksonville) with no-cost interment for eligible veterans.

- **HB 2535 – Veterans and Gold Star Family Property Tax Relief Act** ,  If enacted, Purple Heart recipients would receive up to **$5,000/year** property tax exemption on their primary residence, effective **January 1, 2027**. ([MO HB 2535](https://documents.house.mo.gov/billtracking/bills261/hlrbillspdf/4245H.06C.pdf))
- **HB 2202 – Lifetime Hunting & Fishing Permits for Purple Heart Recipients** ,  Proposed free combined lifetime hunting and fishing permit for Purple Heart recipients and surviving spouses. ([MO HB 2202](https://documents.house.mo.gov/billtracking/bills261/hlrbillspdf/5228H.01I.pdf))
